Christmas décor and gifts, delivered across India

Toouniq Christmas Market is a small Christmas shop with cute, giftable décor you won't find everywhere. Shop ceramic Christmas mugs like the Capybara Christmas Cup and Penguin Mug, glowing ceramic Christmas houses and tealight candle holders, 3D resin gnomes, Santas and snowmen, cookie and candy holders, printed steel bottles and festive table décor.

Every piece is chosen to make your home feel warm and festive, or to make a thoughtful Christmas gift for family, friends and colleagues. We keep lots of varieties but only a few pieces of each, so each order feels a little bit unique.

Prefer shopping in person? Visit our physical store, Aarna Creations, 529 Mali Galli, opposite Sangli Urban Bank, Sangli, and follow @toouniq on Instagram for new Christmas arrivals and reels.

Ordering is simple: add your favourites to the cart, send the order on WhatsApp and pay with Google Pay or any UPI app. We ship from Maharashtra to all states in India.

Frequently asked questions

How do I order from Toouniq Christmas Market?

Tap Buy now on any piece (or Add to cart for several), fill in your name, mobile and address, and tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order arrives in our WhatsApp chat with the items, shipping and total. You can pay by UPI right away or after we confirm.

How do I pay?

Pay with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app to our UPI ID clearmithun@okaxis, or scan the QR code in your cart. Then share the payment screenshot in the same WhatsApp chat.

What are the shipping charges?

Shipping is ₹120 per kg within Maharashtra and ₹150 per kg for all other states in India. The cart works it out for you and rounds the weight up to the next full kg.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We ship our Christmas décor to every state in India. Choose your state in the cart to see the shipping charge.

Do you have a physical store?

Yes. Our physical store is Aarna Creations, 529 Mali Galli, opposite Sangli Urban Bank, Sangli, Maharashtra. Call 98506 66440 or 90968 10210. Open it in Google Maps and message us on WhatsApp before visiting. Follow @toouniq on Instagram for new arrivals.

How long does delivery take?

We pack and dispatch within 1–2 working days. Delivery usually takes 2–4 days in Maharashtra, 4–7 days in other states and 7–10 days to the North-East, J&K, Ladakh and the islands. Tap “Check delivery by pincode” next to any Buy button to see your dates. We confirm the exact dispatch date on WhatsApp.

Why is stock limited?

We keep many varieties but only a few pieces of each, so popular items like the Capybara Christmas Cup and the gnomes sell out fast. New Christmas arrivals are added regularly.

Which Christmas houses light up?

The Nordic Reindeer LED Ceramic Christmas House and the Santa & Snowman Glowing Christmas Houses have a light inside. The Burgundy Brick European House and the Olive House are tealight candle holders that glow when you place a tealight inside.
